SPECIFICATION • ARCHIVAL PROVENANCE

Throughout the golden era of Canadian broadcast engineering, both Ward-Beck Systems and McCurdy Radio Industries specified a signature Dark Blue Baked Enamel for custom studio consoles and rack gear. Unlike standard industrial gray or jet black, this deep midnight navy hue (centered at #161c35) provides superior non-reflective diffusion under harsh overhead fluorescent control room lighting while delivering warm, elegant contrast against cream meter dials and amber pilot lamps.

Authentic Material Properties
  • Substrate: 16-gauge cold-rolled mild steel / annealed 5052-H32 aluminum chassis.
  • Chemistry: Cross-linked thermoset polyester alkyd baked enamel (oven-cured at 180°C).
  • Texture Profile: Fine stippled / light orange-peel stipple preventing specular glare.
  • Sheen: Low luster semi-matte (20° gloss meter rating of 18–22 GU).
  • Solvent Resistance: High resistance to broadcast studio cleaning solvents and oils.
