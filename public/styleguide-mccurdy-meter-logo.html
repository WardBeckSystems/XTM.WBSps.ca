03 / Typography & Letterform Anatomy

Optical sizing and casing specifications for the "McCURDY" logotype

Letterform Casing & Scale Architecture

The trademark McCurdy meter logotype uses a distinct hybrid casing format where the letter 'c' is rendered in lowercase while all other letters are uppercase:

McCURDY
Cap (100%) Lower (78%) Cap (100%) Cap (100%) Cap (100%) Cap (100%) Cap (100%)
  • Leading 'M': Standard capital letter, defines the $100\%$ cap height reference.
  • Lowercase 'c': Optical height is strictly $78\%$ of cap height, aligned firmly to the baseline.
  • Trailing 'CURDY': Capital letters matching the exact height, stroke weight, and optical density of the initial 'M'.
  • Tracking / Kerning: $+0.04\text{em}$ to $+0.06\text{em}$ letter spacing to prevent ink fill during precision silk-screen printing.

Approved Font Families & Fallbacks

Original McCurdy instrument faces utilized hand-drafted Swiss grotesk letterforms derived from classic Haas/Linotype Helvetica:

Priority Font Family Weight Target Environment
Primary Helvetica Neue, Helvetica 700 (Bold) Web / SVG Master Vector
Alternate 1 Arial, -apple-system 700 (Bold) Universal Cross-Platform
Alternate 2 Univers 65 / Highway Grotesk Bold High-Resolution Print Reproduction
Proportion Rule: Never render the wordmark as all-caps MCCURDY or standard sentence-case Mccurdy. The small-c baseline hybrid is the authentic proprietary mark.

05 / Responsive Scaling & Legibility Rules

Why the little text goes away on small meter dials

The Optical Legibility Problem at Sub-Pixel Scale

On a physical 3.5" to 4.5" audio meter faceplate (e.g. McCurdy SA14023), the entire brand cartouche is printed only 18 mm to 22 mm wide. The sub-line RADIO INDUSTRIES LIMITED is printed at less than 0.5 mm height.

In digital screen rendering, when the badge is scaled below 160 px width (such as on an 88 px meter dial badge), the 12.5 px font scales down to under 1.8 px. At this size:

  • Font glyph stems collapse into fractional pixels.
  • Anti-aliasing produces a muddy, illegible grey smear across the pill bar.
  • Visual clutter distracts from the needle and audio scale reading.
Engineering Rule: When badge width is < 160 px (scale < 0.35×), the text RADIO INDUSTRIES LIMITED must be omitted. The solid pill bar remains underneath 'cCURDY' providing a crisp, sharp grounding baseline.
